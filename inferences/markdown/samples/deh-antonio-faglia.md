### Biographical note

## Português

**Antonio Faglia** (nome chinês: Fa Ngan-to Cheng-Hio), jesuíta de Itália, Padre.

Nasceu em 1663-11-03 em [Brescia](Q6221). Entrou na Companhia de Jesus em [Génova](Q1449) (Genoa) em 1683-01-27. Embarcou para o Oriente no navio *?* (1692). Chegou a [Macau](Q14773) (1694-07-11). Residiu em [Cantão](Q16572) (Guangzhou) (1695), [Shantung](Q43407) (Shandong) (1697), [Shantung](Q43407) (Shandong) (1700), [Macau](Q14773) (1704). Fez a profissão de votos em [Sungkiang](Q11104671) (Songjiang Fu) em 1706-08-29. Faleceu em 1706-12-15 em [Kiashing (Kian-hing) fou, Tchö-kiang](Q58178) (Jiaxing). Residiu em [Pequim](Q956) (Beijing) (>1694-07-11), [Tchö-kiang](Q16967) (Zhejiang) (>1695), [Tchö-kiang](Q16967) (Zhejiang) (>1705).

## English

**Antonio Faglia** (Chinese name: Fa Ngan-to Cheng-Hio), Jesuit from Itália, priest (Father).

Born 1663-11-03 in [Brescia](Q6221). Entered the Society of Jesus in [Génova](Q1449) (Genoa) on 1683-01-27. Embarked for the East on the ship *?* (1692). Arrived in [Macau](Q14773) (1694-07-11). Resided in [Cantão](Q16572) (Guangzhou) (1695), [Shantung](Q43407) (Shandong) (1697), [Shantung](Q43407) (Shandong) (1700), [Macau](Q14773) (1704). Professed vows vows in [Sungkiang](Q11104671) (Songjiang Fu) on 1706-08-29. Died 1706-12-15 in [Kiashing (Kian-hing) fou, Tchö-kiang](Q58178) (Jiaxing). Resided in [Pequim](Q956) (Beijing) (>1694-07-11), [Tchö-kiang](Q16967) (Zhejiang) (>1695), [Tchö-kiang](Q16967) (Zhejiang) (>1705).
