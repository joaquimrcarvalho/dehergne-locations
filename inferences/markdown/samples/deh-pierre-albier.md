## English

**Pierre Albier**, Jesuit from França, priest (Father).

Born 1620 in [Limousin](Q1190). Entered the Society of Jesus in (unknown) on 1644. Professed vows vows in [Agen](Q6625) on 1654-07-12. Embarked for the East on the ship *?* (1654-08-30). (para Lisboa?) Resided in [Bordeaux](Q1479) (1654-08-30), [Lisboa](Q597) (Lisbon) (1655-03-23). Embarked for the East on the ship *S. Francisco* (1655-03-23). (original: Capitaina; corrigido segundo Wicky) Resided in [Macau](Q14773) (1657-02), [Tonquim](Q622778) (Tonkin) (1658), [Tonquim](Q622778) (Tonkin) (1663), [Sião (Tailândia)](Q869) (Thailand) (1664-07-29). Died 1665-02-20 in [Sião](Q869) (Thailand). Resided in [Jacarta](Q3630) (<1665-02-20).
