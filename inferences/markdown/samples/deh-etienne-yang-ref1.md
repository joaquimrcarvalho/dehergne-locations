### Biographical note

## Português

**Aloys Kao**.

Nasceu em 1732-01-19 em [Pequim](Q956) (Beijing). Foi ordenado padre em [Paris](Q90) em 1763-05-28. Residiu em [Lorient](Q71724) (1765-02-01). Embarcou para o Oriente no navio *Duc de Choiseul* (1765-02-01). Residiu em [Macau](Q14773) (1765-07-02), [Cantão](Q16572) (Guangzhou) (1765-11-17). Chegou a [Pequim](Q956) (Beijing) (1766-02-02).

Esteve ligado a [Christophe de Beaumont](deh-etienne-yang-ref4) (Ordena (in), 1763-05-28), [Etienne Yang](deh-etienne-yang) (Companion, 1765-02-01).

## English

**Aloys Kao**.

Born 1732-01-19 in [Pequim](Q956) (Beijing). Ordained priest in [Paris](Q90) on 1763-05-28. Resided in [Lorient](Q71724) (1765-02-01). Embarked for the East on the ship *Duc de Choiseul* (1765-02-01). Resided in [Macau](Q14773) (1765-07-02), [Cantão](Q16572) (Guangzhou) (1765-11-17). Arrived in [Pequim](Q956) (Beijing) (1766-02-02).

Connected to [Christophe de Beaumont](deh-etienne-yang-ref4) (Ordena (in), 1763-05-28), [Etienne Yang](deh-etienne-yang) (Companion, 1765-02-01).
