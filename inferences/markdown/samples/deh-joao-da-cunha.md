### Biographical note

## Português

**João da Cunha**, jesuíta de Portugal, Padre.

Embarcou para o Oriente no navio *[Para a China]>1755:<1758* (). (entre 1755 e 1758) Residiu em [Lisboa](Q597) (Lisbon). Nasceu em 1731-09-09 em [Coimbra](Q45412). Entrou na Companhia de Jesus em [Lisboa](Q597) (Lisbon) em 1747-11-23.

## English

**João da Cunha**, Jesuit from Portugal, priest (Father).

Embarked for the East on the ship *[Para a China]>1755:<1758* (). (entre 1755 e 1758) Resided in [Lisboa](Q597) (Lisbon). Born 1731-09-09 in [Coimbra](Q45412). Entered the Society of Jesus in [Lisboa](Q597) (Lisbon) on 1747-11-23.

### Notes
**dehergne 231**. Cunha, João da (port.) P. 231
N. Coïmbre, 9 sept. 1731 (Lus. 49, 55).
E. 23 nov. 1747, Lisbonne (Lus. 41, 98 et 49, 55).
Emb. pour la Chine, 1755-1758 (W 2101 et p. 426).,
emprisonné à Lisbonne.,
   redemande la Chine comme mathématicien (Krahl, 132).
Un autre de ce nom est N. en 1702 (dit W p. 421).,
   l'un d'eux a fait ses V. à Rome, 10 août 1767
   (Hisp. 34, 415) et se retrouve à Rome en 1775.
**bibliografia wicky-1967-421**. No. 2101: born in Coimbra in 1702, entered in 1747,
belonged to the “Matheseos deputati” as a friar in 1754,
destined for China, lived in exile in Italy in 1767 as a
priest and former missionary to China (Lus. 49,106;
Tms. 41, 98-99).
