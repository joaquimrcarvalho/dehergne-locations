# Formatting rules for `ls$dehergne/NNN/obs`

- Merge consecutive `ls$dehergne/NNN/obs=` entries for the same person into one block.
- Wrap the obs text in triple quotes immediately after the equals sign: `ls$dehergne/NNN/obs="""..."""`.
- Join any fragmented text into a single paragraph before rewrapping.
- Reflow lines to about 80 characters (ASCII) without changing wording or citations.
- Put each event marker on its own line (e.g., `N. ...`, `E. ...`, `Emb. ...`, `Arr. ...`, `V. ...`, `M. ...`, `P. ...`, `v. ...`).
- Preserve all references, citations, accents, and punctuation exactly; only adjust whitespace/line breaks.
- Keep the original indentation level aligned with surrounding `ls$` lines.
- Do not alter other fields or add/remove content—only reformat the obs text.
